Terms of use

These terms describe the current use of IntraNest, a self-hosted tool for teams.

Updated October 1, 2026

Use within your team

Your organization chooses who can reach its server and who receives the shared access key. Only share information you are allowed to share with those people.

Your server and content

The server host controls availability, backups, updates, file-size limits, and chat retention. Shared-key holders can read, add, and delete links, files, and chat messages.

Responsible use

Do not use IntraNest to break the law, disrupt another system, or share content without permission. Follow your organization’s policies and the software license that accompanies your copy.

Availability

IntraNest is provided as self-hosted software. The project maintainer does not operate your intranet server or promise that it will be available, backed up, or free of errors.
